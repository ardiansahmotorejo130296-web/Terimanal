## 2024-05-18 - [Jetpack Compose Accessibility - Touch Targets & Semantics]
**Learning:** In Jetpack Compose, wrapping `Icon` elements that have `Modifier.clickable` with an `IconButton` is a cleaner way to ensure a minimum 48dp accessible touch target size while keeping visual elements small. Additionally, using `Modifier.clickable` directly on structural elements (like `Row` or `Box` or `Text`) without explicit `role = Role.Button` and an `onClickLabel` leads to poor screen reader context, omitting important interaction cues for visually impaired users.
**Action:** When adding interactive elements, always use `IconButton` (or standard `Button`) for icons. When `Modifier.clickable` must be used on non-button elements, always explicitly import `androidx.compose.ui.semantics.Role` and provide both `onClickLabel` and `role = Role.Button` in the modifier configuration.
