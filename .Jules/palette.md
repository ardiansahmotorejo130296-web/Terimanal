## 2026-09-15 - Accessibility Improvements in TerminalInputComponent
**Learning:** In Jetpack Compose, applying `.clickable()` without explicit semantics on non-button elements (like `Row` or `Box`) makes them inaccessible to screen readers. Also, wrapping a standard 24dp `Icon` with an `IconButton` is a clean way to ensure the minimum 48dp touch target size.
**Action:** Always provide explicit `onClickLabel` and `role = Role.Button` semantics when making structural elements clickable, and use `IconButton` over bare `Icon` with `.clickable` for standalone actions.
